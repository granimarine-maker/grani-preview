Main particulars

  • LOA: 14.70 m
  • Beam: 7.80 m
  • Depth: 2.50 m
  • Light draft: 1.00 m
  • Crew: 2 + 2
  • Clearance: 0.90 m
  • Designed deck cargo capacity: 14 t

Machinery

  • Two Volvo Penta D7C Turbo engines, 199 hp each
  • Twin Disc / ZF gearboxes
  • Two 600 mm propellers
  • 12 kW diesel generator

Deck & systems

  • Deck crane options available; reference configuration up to 5.7 t lifting capacity
  • Wheelhouse arranged for two crew and two surveyors
  • Navigation suite includes radar, GPS, NAVTEX, compass and echo sounder
  • Hull, propulsion and deck arrangements can be adapted to project needs
