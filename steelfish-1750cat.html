Baird Maritime · Awards 2021

Best OSV – DSV

Baird Maritime named K&C One its 2021 Best OSV – DSV, recognising a compact steel-catamaran design that integrates an unusually extensive diving, lifting and deck-equipment package.

Read the independent vessel review →

Machinery & performance

  • Two Cummins QSB 6.7 diesel engines, 260 kW each
  • Two fixed-pitch propellers in Grani-designed nozzles
  • Diesel generators: 120 kW and 46 kW
  • Maximum speed: 11 kn
  • Service speed: 9 kn
  • Bollard pull: 10 t

Deck & diving equipment

  • Heila crane with 6.3 t lifting capacity
  • Stern A-frame with 7 t capacity
  • 30 t anchor-handling and towing winch
  • Four-point mooring system
  • 1.2 × 1.0 m moonpool
  • Container locks and stern roller
  • Dive-control unit, low-pressure compressor and two high-pressure compressors
