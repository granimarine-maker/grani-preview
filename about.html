About Grani

Independent maritime engineering and consultancy from Odesa, supporting shipowners, yards, contractors and investors since 1989.

View projectsContact us

Built around practical maritime experience.

Grani Ltd was established in 1989 as an independent private company. For more than 35 years, the company has supported marine, shipbuilding and ship-manning projects for clients in Ukraine and internationally.

Our work combines naval architecture, ship design, newbuilding support and marine consultancy. Depending on the project, we coordinate specialists across hull structures, machinery, ship systems, electrical engineering, automation, surveying and commercial support.

Who we support

  • Shipowners and operators
  • Shipyards and contractors
  • Banks and leasing companies
  • Investors and project developers
  • Law firms, insurers and other maritime stakeholders

How we work

Grani can act as designer, engineering coordinator, local representative or owner-side technical adviser. The scope is defined around the client’s decision, the vessel and the stage of the project.
