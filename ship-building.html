From concept to delivery

Grani supports clients in identifying suitable shipyards, preparing technical and commercial information, arranging inspections and meetings, coordinating project follow-up and providing technical surveillance during construction.

How we support clients

  • Feasibility and pre-contract support
  • Shipyard search and technical pre-qualification
  • Tender and proposal comparison
  • Local market and regulatory guidance
  • Technical liaison between owner, designer and yard
  • Construction follow-up and inspection support
  • Problem solving during build
  • Delivery and close-out support

Local knowledge, international perspective

Based in Odesa, Grani combines knowledge of the Ukrainian maritime and industrial environment with experience working for international owners, contractors and investors. The objective is to reduce project risk, improve transparency and keep the owner informed throughout the build process.
