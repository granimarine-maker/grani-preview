Main particulars

  • LOA: 18.00 m
  • Beam: 9.00 m
  • Depth at sides: 2.90 m
  • Aft draft: 1.90 m
  • Displacement: approximately 237 t
  • Trial speed: 9.4 kn ± 0.5 kn
  • Trial deck payload: 40 t
  • Bollard pull ahead: 9 t

Machinery & capacities

  • Main engines: 2 × Weichai WD12C400-21, 400 hp @ 2100 rpm
  • Gearboxes: DC 3:1
  • Two fixed-pitch steel propellers, 1200 mm
  • Fuel oil: 43 m³ total
  • Fresh water: 24.5 m³ total
  • Water ballast: 29.6 m³ total

Classification basis
