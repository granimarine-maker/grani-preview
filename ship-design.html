Engineering capability

Grani assembles project teams according to the technical scope, drawing on experienced naval architects, hull designers, mechanical engineers, systems specialists and electrical / automation designers. The company’s project experience spans workboats, tugs, fishing vessels, Ro-Ro ships, tankers, supply vessels, research craft and specialised conversions.

Typical scope

  • Concept and contract design
  • General arrangement and outline studies
  • Classification documentation
  • Detailed structural and outfitting design
  • Production drawings and cutting information
  • Ship operational documentation
  • Conversion, modernisation and reclassification design
  • Interior and exterior design support
  • Construction cost studies
  • On-site technical support and supervision

Design tools

Project teams have worked with AutoCAD, Rhinoceros, 3ds Max, TRIBON and specialised production-design software. Software selection is matched to the project and client workflow.
