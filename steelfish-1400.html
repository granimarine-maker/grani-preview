Operating modes

Main particulars

  • LOA: 14.90 m
  • Length between perpendiculars: 13.45 m
  • Beam: 5.00 m
  • Draft: 1.20 m
  • Displacement: 35 t
  • Carrying capacity: at least 5 t / 7.5 m³
  • Crew: 2
  • Berths: 6
  • Fuel endurance: at least 192 hours
  • Cargo hold: 10 m³, insulated

Machinery & construction

  • Fully welded steel hull with transverse framing
  • Watertight subdivision arranged to meet applicable stability requirements
  • Main engine: Perkins Sabre M215C, 215 hp @ 2500 rpm
  • Fixed-pitch propeller with remote engine control from the wheelhouse
  • Navigation and fish-finding equipment configured to customer requirements
