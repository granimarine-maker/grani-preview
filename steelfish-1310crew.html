Main particulars

  • LOA: 13.10 m (18.00 m variant available)
  • Beam: 4.00 m
  • Draft: 1.10 m steel / 0.85 m aluminium
  • Cruising speed: 11 kn steel / 22 kn aluminium

Machinery

  • Main engines: 2 × Volvo Penta D7C TA
  • Power: 195 kW × 2 @ 2300 rpm
  • Gearboxes: ZF325IV × 2
  • Drive: twin propellers
  • Reference generator: Fischer Panda 6000 / 200V / 50Hz

Arrangement

  • Passenger saloon for 16
  • Separate galley, shower and toilet compartment
  • Forward crew cabin with two berths
  • Cargo deck space for utility work
